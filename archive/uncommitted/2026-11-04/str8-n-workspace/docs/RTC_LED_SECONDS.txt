RTC physical seconds display - enabled November 1, 2026

Live-qualified and enabled: 2205, 2512, 2604.
2609 is owner-held, unresponsive after a loader mismatch, with RAM restoration
pending. The owner declined a CPU reset. Do not open its port or clear its
restoration/owner-hold guards without a new owner recovery instruction.

During each 62-second drift collection, the eight LEDs show BCD seconds:
four red LEDs = tens in binary; four green LEDs = units in binary.
At actual RTC seconds 00, all-red/all-green frames alternate about every
100 ms. This is a binary LED indication, not a seven-segment numeral.
The 794-byte temporary RAM collector reads the RTC without setting it.
Nine evenly spaced accepted readings feed the drift campaign; all polling
observations and LED readback evidence remain in observations.jsonl.

Saved-data audits verify the exact uploaded/restored image, allowed serial
commands, RAM backups, both zero-second flash phases, LED/control restoration,
NIST reference quality, CPU/USB identity and original monitor prompt.
No clock, trim, PF acknowledgement/history, EEPROM, flash, firmware, saved
settings or baseline changes are part of this mode. EEPROM history is retained
but is not freshly scanned by this transport.

Native 2512 uses its established bounded M RAM transport. Legacy 2205 and
2604 use L/S-records. Never use M RAM writes on 2604. Do not generalize bare
L/S19 loading to BASE1 monitors: bare L can select the raw BIN loader.
The initial mismatch changed two 2512 low-RAM bytes; they were restored and
verified against its hash-checked owner backup. The 2609 mismatch remains
blocked and has not been reset or power-cycled.
