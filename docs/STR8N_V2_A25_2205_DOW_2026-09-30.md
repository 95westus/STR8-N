# Alpha25 EDU RTC weekday: board 2205

Board: W65C02SXB 2205 with EDU on COM3, 2026-09-30. The E-only update adds a three-letter weekday before the two-digit date and 24-hour time:

```text
STR8-N 2.0a25 B3 65C02
ABI 65C02 | 816E | 816N-VEC
EDU KIT       DETECTED
  RTC         Wed 26-09-30 18:40:16
B3>
```

The weekday comes from MCP79411 `RTCWKDAY` (1=Sun through 7=Sat on this board). The first trial mapped 1=Mon and the physical RESET displayed `Thu` for 2026-09-30. The second guarded E-only update corrected the table. Neither update wrote RTC registers.

Both transitions used two independent, full 8192-byte E/F readbacks, exact-image RAM updater gates, host flash-model tests, on-board E verification, complete post-update E/F readback, and a physical RESET. The first and final captures are under `BUILD/board/a25-dow/` and `BUILD/board/a25-dow-mapping/`.

`make v2-a25-rtc-check` passed after the final source change. It covers seven weekday values, invalid DOW, RTC validity and advancement, 12/24-hour conversion, absent bus, and startup placement. The F sector and published ABI addresses are unchanged.

Microchip specifies that `RTCWKDAY` counts 1?7, while the meaning of each number is user-defined. The board's Sunday-first assignment was established by the live date/display check, not by a chip-defined default. The three-letter English names follow Unicode CLDR; ISO 8601 does not prescribe English weekday abbreviations.
