# Board 2205 alpha25 EDU status display update

Date: 2026-09-30. W65C02SXB board 2205 with EDU attached, FT245 COM3
serial `A10MPUPNA` at 115200 baud. This was a guarded **B3:E-only** update
of the installed alpha25 RTC extension. The F-sector binary, reset vectors,
public ROM/RAM ABI, and S/R/T extension remained byte-identical.

Two fresh full `D E000 FFFF` readbacks matched each other and the prior
installed alpha25 image, SHA-256
`c00f0ad1a7a1521008c508e4a3bfb84f6d62737dd2bcef565174434aa719ce4b`.
The [builder](../tools/build_v2_a25_edu_status_update.py) embedded exact
old E, new E, and required F images. The [host flash test](../tools/test_v2_a25_edu_status_update.py)
passed exact E/F preflight gates, cancellation, verified E programming, and
B0/B2/F preservation.

The board updater offered `B3:E exact; TYPE Y to repair>` only after the
exact live E and required F comparisons. After confirmation it reported
`B3:E VERIFIED; RESET`. Its automatic monitor restart printed:

```text
STR8-N 2.0a25 B3 65C02
ABI 65C02 | 816E | 816N-VEC
EDU KIT       DETECTED
  RTC         26-09-30 18:28:13
B3>
```

A separate full E/F readback matched the source-built new E and unchanged F
byte for byte, SHA-256
`0b8dce25c9adfe8a106a4fa1b2df0edcb0ce965738aa813675f79dc17cacfdbd`.
The owner pressed physical RESET during a receive-only capture. The board
again printed the status layout, with RTC time `18:29:04`, before `B3>`.

Owner-local serial logs, S19, readback BINs, manifest, and result receipt are
under `BUILD/board/a25-edu-status/`. `EDU KIT INCONCLUSIVE` for an invalid,
stopped, or unresponsive RTC passed host execution; this branch was not
exercised by removing the EDU during the board run. No EEPROM, SPI RAM, or
crypto probe was added or claimed.
