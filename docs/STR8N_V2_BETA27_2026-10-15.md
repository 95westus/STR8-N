# Beta27: consolidate utilities in B2

Beta27 (generation 39) moves the three MAINT sectors from B1:8000-AFFF to
B2:8000-AFFF and shifts the prior B2 A-E contents one sector right. The target
board is 2609. Its CLOCK flash record must already be absent: CLK is retained
in SPI SRAM, and no SRAM contents are changed by this migration.

```text
     8  9  A  B  C  D  E  F
B1   .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .
B2   S  +  +  S  S  +  F  F
B3   I  I  A  B* J  J  X  R
```

| Component | New flash location |
| --- | --- |
| MAINT 2.2 | B2:8000-AFFF |
| SRAM 1.2 transport | B2:B000-BEF9 |
| WORK 1.2 record | B2:C000-D4A2 |
| EDU 1.2 record | B2:D580-D7FE |
| Status/storage formatter | B2:D800-E1FF |
| Battery-age and flash-wear helper | B2:E200-EBFF |
| Local/board formatter | B2:F000-F6FF |
| VERSION helper | B2:F800-FFFF |

The remaining bytes in firmware allocations are reserved padding. AUTO keeps
its existing below-E000 scan limit and excludes D800 onward in B2. B1 is now
available to user allocations. B0, fixed B3:F, service ABI, application RAM
limits and all board identity/name/timezone/battery records remain unchanged.

`tools/build_v2_beta27.py` builds a separate package under BUILD/v2-beta27.
The SRAM manager's CRC/read references and all four relocated transport-copy
fragments are updated. The status loader and local formatter loader use the
new addresses. MAINT 2.2 updates transfer scanning, map descriptions and sealed
asset checks, including CRC iteration wrapping past B2:FFFF. Shared B3:E entry
addresses remain unchanged so retained beta26 A can use the relocated services.
The candidate slot B reports beta27; A remains byte-identical beta26.

`tools/deploy_v2_beta27.py` accepts only a fresh twice-verified backup with the
qualified beta26 B2 layout, a free B2:F, original MAINT in B1 and no other B1
contents. Unexpected allocations refuse installation. It constructs the entire
target offline, then prepares fourteen guarded sector updates. B2 copies go
from high to low; EDU, WORK, SRAM and MAINT headers commit only after their complete
records verify. B1's three occupied sectors are erased only after MAINT is
committed at its destination. The inactive beta27 monitor commits last. Every
planned erase attempt is added to the sector wear journal; fixed F is untouched.

This is a multi-sector layout conversion, not an atomic swap of the entire
layout. An interruption can leave shared utilities unavailable until the
backup-bound recovery is completed; fixed F and the active beta26 slot are
preserved. Do not interrupt the RAM installer. It halts for a physical RESET
after device verification.

`tools/test_v2_beta27.py` executes both monitor slots against the new layout,
including TIME/VERSION, map CRCs, preserved SRAM loading and CLK launch,
WORK/EDU/MAINT, AUTO reservations, EDU OFF, and invalid-slot fallback.
`tools/test_v2_beta27_migration.py` rehearses the exact installer, checks stale
preimage/corrupt-payload refusals, and guards MAINT source removal and monitor
commit ordering. Physical qualification additionally requires full post-reset
flash readback and retained SRAM/RTC/EEPROM comparisons.

Offline qualification passed for beta27 B and byte-identical beta26 A,
including map views, AUTO reservations, corrupt-slot fallback, EDU OFF and
relocated asset CRC/header rejection. The exact physical-backup fixture also
passed using the board's archived CLK image. A separate model-only round trip
copied relocated WORK through SPI SRAM into B1:8000 with an exact payload,
retained source and no sector erases.

The captured-state test seeds the RTC from the board's before-capture register
snapshot. Its initial October 8 fixture date correctly triggered the battery
history's backward-clock protection; correcting the fixture to the captured
October 16 UTC date removed that artificial discontinuity. No hardware data
was changed by that model run.
