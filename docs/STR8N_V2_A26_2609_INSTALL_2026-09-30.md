# Board 2609: alpha25 to alpha26 without host flash readback

Board 2609 is the W65C816SXB on COM8, FT245 serial `A10MPQXCA`. The owner
requested an alpha26 installation without a flash readback and expects no EDU
EEPROM. The serial installer verified the port identity and an alpha25
`G F007` software reentry before loading each RAM updater.

The [board builder](../tools/build_v2_a26_2609_guarded_ef.py) used the
archived alpha25 E/F install targets and their prior board verification logs.
It did not read flash from the board. The [flash-model test](../tools/test_v2_a26_2609_guarded_ef.py)
passed the exact-image gates, E-before-F ordering, verification, refusal,
cancellation, and preservation of B0/B2. The two updater S19 files and
transaction logs are in `BUILD/board/a26-2609/`.

| E | `2114c567136f4d8d79a2836a26e78d39e7308e35097c6eb6e87eeb77368fdf98` | `0e8e8e2295b9d3b51b09ab3512377a366d758619ab1524fe9ace936604853492` |
| F | `651fecf38f5ad1a82bd1254e64ea0361d92053ca0f6226a0d3daa2d7aa74b2a3` | `53f1ef4974c104007be03b4dbeedb1f6c3c6fe7a5c4038c30efb80c0f8a3ea69` |

The [serial installer](../tools/install_v2_a26_2609_guarded_ef.py) performed
E first. Its on-board preflight reported `B3:E exact; TYPE Y to repair>`;
after confirmation, it reported `B3:E VERIFIED; RESET` and restarted a25.
The F updater then required the staged a26 E sector and the archived a25 F
sector. Its preflight reported `B3:F exact; TYPE Y to update>`; after
confirmation, it reported `B3:F VERIFIED; RESET` and restarted a26.

The installed banner and a separate `G F007` software reentry both printed:

```text
STR8-N 2.0a26 B3 65C816
ABI 65C02 | 816E | 816N-VEC
EDU KIT       INCONCLUSIVE
  EEPROM      UNAVAILABLE
    SETTINGS  UNREADABLE
B3>
```

No EEPROM write was attempted. With EEPROM unavailable, a26 leaves autostart
off and remains at the monitor prompt. B3:E `$EFF0-$EFFF` now belongs to the
free E-sector tail; the previous all-zero 256-byte E margin was replaced by
erased `$FF` bytes. No additional EEPROM uses were enabled.

No host flash dump or host byte-for-byte readback was performed. The recorded
sector identities rest on the board's exact-image preflight and post-write
verification messages. A physical RESET was not observed in this session;
the post-write software restart and separate software reentry both succeeded.
EEPROM read/write behavior still requires a board with the MCP79411 attached.
