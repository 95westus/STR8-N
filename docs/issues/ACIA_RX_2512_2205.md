# Issue: W65C51N ACIA receive absent on boards 2205 and 2512

ID: STR8N-001

Status: **Deferred — meter, logic probe, or oscilloscope evidence required**.
Owner action: resume hardware diagnosis when instruments and the USB-to-TTL
adapter are available. Do not mark the backup-console gate passed from host
models or transmit-only tests.
The current W65C02SXB FT245-only operating claim does not depend on this
issue; ACIA receive and fallback must remain unadvertised until close criteria
are met.

## Observed behavior

- Board 2205: the direct RAM probe transmitted its 19200 8N1 banner, but a
  board TXD-to-RXD loopback produced no received-byte report. A programmed
  W65C51N reset followed by control `$1F` and command `$0B` did not change
  the result. Status remained `$70`, with RDRF clear. The Waveshare cable's
  green TXD-to-blue RXD self-loopback passed.
- Board 2512: the frozen direct probe transmitted the repeating banner to
  COM7 (`BG02PM7ZA`). Sending `A` or `Q` yielded no `ACIA RX $41/$51`, echo,
  or `Q` return. FT245 reported status `$70`. Physical RESET was required to
  exit. The ACIA adapter was absent during the later no-EDU alpha20/alpha21
  installation and cold-power sessions.
- These observations show a working timed transmit path and an unqualified
  receive path. They do not distinguish wiring, signal level, receive clock,
  handshake, board trace, or W65C51N behavior. No cause is established.

Evidence: [2205 diagnosis](../STR8N_V2_ALPHA13_BOARD_TEST_2026-09-23.md#board-2205-acia-receive-diagnosis),
[2512 alpha19 test](../STR8N_V2_ALPHA19_2512_BOARD_TEST_2026-09-24.md#acia-receive-issue),
and [qualification matrix](../STR8N_V2_QUALIFICATION.md).

## Resume with instruments

1. Verify the Waveshare USB TO TTL (C) is set for 5 V logic and has common
   ground with the board. At J4, adapter green TXD goes to pin 5 RXD, blue
   RXD to pin 7 TXD, black GND to pin 2 VSS. Verify J4 pin 9 CTSB is low for
   the probe. Leave the adapter VCC lead disconnected.
2. While sending a repeating known byte at 19200 8N1, measure voltage and
   transitions at the adapter TXD lead, J4 pin 5, and the W65C51N RXD net.
   Record instrument setup, idle level, bit timing, and whether transitions
   reach each point. Do not infer signal integrity from wire color alone.
3. Measure W65C51N RXC activity at J4 pin 10 and the relevant active-low
   handshake inputs, especially CTSB at J4 pin 9. Record supply and ground
   levels during the test.
4. Run the exact direct ACIA RAM probe again and capture FT245 status/RX
   reports alongside the measured waveforms or logic traces. If physical RX
   and clock inputs are valid but RDRF remains clear, isolate board/chip
   behavior before changing the STR8-N receive driver.

## Close criteria

Close this issue only after physical receive is explained and either repaired
or explicitly ruled out for the supported hardware. To qualify the backup
console, demonstrate received bytes, echo, `Q` return, automatic fallback
selection, and a transfer through ACIA on the actual board. Preserve the
instrument captures, board identity, wiring, firmware identity, commands,
and exact results. If repair is impossible, document a release-scope decision
separately; do not label the backup console qualified.
