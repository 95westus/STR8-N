# Evidence omitted from this export

This edition omits selected board-specific acceptance plans and validation
receipts, including ones inherited from the public base. Their adapter IDs,
host port assignments and detailed transaction evidence are unnecessary to
reproduce the current payloads. Existing public history and every original
archive remain unchanged. Private captures stay private.

MEMORY_ACCEPTANCE_SUMMARY.md preserves supported region sizes, earlier trial
outcome and qualification limits without raw captures or board inventory.
Current reconstruction receipts and offline reports are generated during the
build; they are distinct from physical board acceptance.
