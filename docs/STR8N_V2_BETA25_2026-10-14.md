## Compatibility

The earlier 005 candidate changed the byte-derived F identity even though the
monitor ABI stayed compatible. Beta25 explicitly retains the proven generation
35 ABI-family token. This is distinct from exact firmware identity, which is
bound by full F SHA256 and a separately recorded canonical CRC32.

The builder proves that all F entries referenced by the old monitor retain
their addresses, all J entries/CRC remain fixed, and the RAM worker and vector
symbols and actual copied bytes are identical. The F instruction bytes are
identical to the qualified 005 candidate; only the four compatibility bytes
change. Slot CRC, ABI, address, commit and generation checks are retained.
Unrelated compatibility cookies still refuse. Existing MAINT already targets
the retained ABI and remains byte-identical; no B1 update is needed.

Both beta25 slot templates are built for release use, but this board's plan
writes **only B**. The only monitor-code change is the same-size beta25 banner.
The beta24 A header, body and CRC are untouched. Shared F contains the guarded
005 recovery path; shared E contains the APPS repair; B2 retains the local-only
TIME/status helpers. CLOCK remains detailed and unchanged.

settings, preference and conservative wear counts; B2:C/D/E for the two sealed
