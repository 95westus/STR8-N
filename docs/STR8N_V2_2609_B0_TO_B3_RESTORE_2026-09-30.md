# Board 2609 - restore original Bank 0 image into Bank 3

Board 2609 is physically W65C816SXB with SST39SF010A (`BF/B5`). This run
restored the original stock Bank 3 image, retained in Bank 0 during alpha24
migration, into Bank 3. It **retained Bank 0**. Bank 1's bounded S/R record
and Bank 2's W65C02SXB guest were not write targets.

## Preflight and image

At the alpha24 `B3>` prompt, complete 32 KiB `D 8000 FFFF` readbacks were
captured and extracted for both banks. Every address appeared in 2,048 rows.

| Image | SHA-256 | Other identity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B3 before restore | `0465acd0a3e39497e026b1df88533990cd20fa11363bb6e57a842731a8dccec9` | alpha24 resident |
| B0 source | `1398d9551f7072ff36203307f55b7e41204f2579ce667515920a2703845eef94` | FNV-1a `280928EC`, matching original B3 measured before migration; RESET `$F818` |

The board-specific RAM restore was assembled from
`tools/wdcmonv2/wdcmonv2str8n-install-2000.asm` with
`W2I_RESTORE_STOCK=1`, `W2I_RESTORE_KEEP_B0=1`, and
`STR8_V2_A24_816=1`. The S19 entry is `$2000`, occupies `$2000-$2BC7`
(3,016 bytes), begins with 816 `SEC/XCE/SEI`, and has SHA-256
`cdb4499fed6e2fccea8fa7c30fb23dad283abbb48e58a776c3223d9f254f2ad8`.
Its linked image contains the B0-preserved success message and no B0-erase
routine or factory-erase prompt. The owner-local S19 and logs are under
`output/qualification/board-2609-restore-2026-09-30/`.

The alpha24 `L` command accepted the S19 with `Entry 2000`. A full monitor
RAM dump then matched all 3,016 linked bytes before execution. `G 2000`
reported `FLASH ID=BF/B5`, `SOURCE B0 FNV1A=280928EC RESET=$F818`, and
`TYPE RESTORE B0 TO B3>`. The host sent that exact token once.

## Result

The RAM tool copied and verified sectors 8-E, then the reset-bearing F
sector. It reported:

```text
COPY/VERIFY B0 -> B3 8-E .......
B3:F LAST .
B0 == B3 WHOLE BANK VERIFIED
B0 PRESERVED; B3 STOCK VERIFIED; PRESS PHYSICAL RESET
```

The user pressed physical RESET while receive-only COM8 capture was active.
The board booted the stock `W65C816SXB + EDU Kit Rev 1.0` native-mode menu
and printed `Init complete.`. The stock menu boot confirms the restored B3
reset path. The RAM tool's exact comparison and final B0/B3 hashes are the
whole-bank proof; no independent post-reset full-chip readback was performed.
The observed peripheral scan belongs to the stock firmware and does not
qualify the absent EDU daughterboard.

Rebuilding the normal alpha24 WDCMONv2 installer after this source change
kept its pinned S19 SHA-256
`66bd1030c2f48444826f03562886fc4828d4047f36d4ba42d1e5f8e5396cbebe`.
The existing v1.35 factory-restore variant also rebuilt successfully.
