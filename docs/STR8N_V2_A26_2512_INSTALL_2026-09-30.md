# Alpha26 installation on board 2512 without EDU

Board 2512 is a W65C02SXB on COM4, FT245 serial `A10MQFLCA`, with no EDU
attached. Its live monitor reported a25. Two independent 8 KiB E/F readbacks
were identical to the canonical a25 image, SHA-256
`8697674a8c2944e4a6d3a6dbe5cdf31d1ab11e037556278e716953f81c7b7d41`.

The board-specific guarded a25-to-a26 RAM updaters passed the flash-model
checks for exact preimages, E/F ordering, write verification, cancellation,
refusal, and preservation of B0/B2. E was installed first and verified on the
board. Its full host readback matched the new E image, SHA-256
`0e8e8e2295b9d3b51b09ab3512377a366d758619ab1524fe9ace936604853492`.
F was then installed and verified on the board. A full E/F host readback was
byte-identical to the canonical a26 image, SHA-256
`62656ba960292c11104d15457b1a0ddb5480011eacdffd7fa1b3079d981127ae`.

Software reentry with `G F007` displayed:

```text
STR8-N 2.0a26 B3 65C02
ABI 65C02 | 816E | 816N-VEC
EDU KIT       INCONCLUSIVE
  EEPROM      UNAVAILABLE
    SETTINGS  UNREADABLE
B3>
```

`C` returned `No config`; `T 3` returned to the monitor. Autostart remains
off when EEPROM is unavailable. The serial transcripts, manifest, and exact
readback files are under `BUILD/board/a26-2512/`. Physical RESET and full
power-off were not tested during this installation.
