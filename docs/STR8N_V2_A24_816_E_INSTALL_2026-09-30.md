# Board 2609: alpha24 Bank 3 E installation

Board 2609 is the W65C816SXB (`SXB6`). Its alpha24 Bank 3 F image was already
installed and read back byte-for-byte before this E-sector run. The owner
identified Bank 2 as the virgin W65C02SXB image and directed that it remain
untouched.

## Preflight and staged image

A complete `D E000 FFFF` dump yielded 512 rows. The live F half matched the
canonical alpha24 F BIN, SHA-256
`43e6ee966963e1cc401986581742cc75b302cd0a02cfaf22965fe7ab8a43ec1a`.
The live E half was uniformly `$00`, SHA-256
`ad7facb2586fc6e966c004d7d1d16b024f5805ff7cb47c7a85dabd8b48892ca7`.
The complete preflight E/F BIN SHA-256 was
`2519c05ff558969b19401a702382a2986219b5e2d5f805d402deaa0552998d15`.

The staged E image preserved the observed bytes at `$E000-$E7FF` and
`$EF00-$EFFF`, including configuration at `$EFF0-$EFFF`. It inserted the
matched alpha24 S/R image only at `$E800-$EEFF`. Staged E SHA-256 is
`49d1e50d3c933d6228bb92f80078b6ac53ec38a1d865f91e84e7cc5ed0da37bf`.
The board-specific E S19 SHA-256 is
`aa19e207e5f20ef6f5f945c4ebaf180fd54b6fd736e2b7f0de5c97283f6c97c8`.
The normal alpha24 `I E000 EFFF` path is protected for resident Bank 3, as
confirmed by the host flash model. It was not used on the board.

## Guarded RAM installation and readback

The board-specific RAM updater S19, SHA-256
`a2a68a1ce876e6bd584f29b6c14e22eaa7433a6de8dc5da10c26f9d0a3997ada`,
loaded at `$2000-$41BF`. It compared the full live E sector with the captured
all-zero preimage before offering `TYPE Y to repair`. After confirmation, it
reported `B3:E VERIFIED` and returned to `B3>`. The flash model passed the
exact-old-image gate, mismatch and cancel refusal, new-image verification,
and B0/B2/F preservation checks.

An independent complete `D E000 FFFF` readback matched the staged E image
and canonical F image byte-for-byte. The post-install E/F SHA-256 is
`5864227879b4298ace8d9c5a6d637cda1347a6c6b4c6ebbdf0505555b58f535c`.
The preserved E margins were unchanged. `T 3` returned to `B3>` without an
`SR unavailable` error. `J3` then restarted the monitor and reached
`STR8-N 2.0a24 B3 65C816` at `B3>`.

The updater selected and wrote only Bank 3:E. Bank 2 was not independently
dumped in this run. The broader native/EDU checks remain open. The later
physical power cycle is recorded below.

## Post-E cold power cycle

After installation, the owner removed and restored power during a receive-only
COM8 capture. The port disconnected and reconnected; no serial bytes were
transmitted. The returning board printed `STR8-N 2.0a24 B3 65C816`, the ABI
line, and `B3>`. A subsequent read-only `D E800 E807` returned
`53 52 01 01 18 00 00 00`, and `T 3` returned to `B3>` without an
`SR unavailable` message. This qualifies post-E cold boot and extension
discovery, not an S/R save/restore operation.
