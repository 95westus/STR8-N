# Board 2609: alpha24-to-alpha25 guarded update without flash readback

Date: 2026-09-30. Board 2609 is the W65C816SXB on COM8, FT245 serial `A10MPQXCA`. The operator requested alpha25 installation through the alpha24 updater path, with no new migrator and no flash readback. Before the update, software reentry printed `STR8-N 2.0a24 B3 65C816`, the established ABI line, and `B3>`.

The [existing alpha25 guarded E/F updater machinery](../tools/build_v2_a25_guarded_ef.py) built two board-specific RAM updaters from the archived, previously verified alpha24 E/F image, SHA-256 `5864227879b4298ace8d9c5a6d637cda1347a6c6b4c6ebbdf0505555b58f535c`. The [2609 builder](../tools/build_v2_a25_2609_guarded_ef.py) retained the board's `$EF00-$EFFF` E-sector bytes and verified that the F configuration/vector tail is unchanged. It produced an alpha25 E target SHA-256 `2114c567136f4d8d79a2836a26e78d39e7308e35097c6eb6e87eeb77368fdf98` and F target SHA-256 `651fecf38f5ad1a82bd1254e64ea0361d92053ca0f6226a0d3daa2d7aa74b2a3`. The E target differs from the canonical alpha25 E BIN because it preserves 2609's existing tail. No migrator was built or run.

The [flash-model test](../tools/test_v2_a25_2609_guarded_ef.py) passed exact old-image gates, E-before-F ordering, cancellation/refusal, verified E/F writes, B0/B2 preservation, and RAM-only handling after an F failure. On hardware:

1. E reported `B3:E exact; TYPE Y to repair>` before confirmation and `B3:E VERIFIED; RESET` after programming. It restarted into alpha24.
2. The [serial installer](../tools/install_v2_a25_2609_guarded_ef.py) verified the exact E-updater S19 and E success in the serial transcript. F then checked the staged E and original F sectors on-device, reported `B3:F exact; TYPE Y to update>`, and after confirmation reported `B3:F VERIFIED; RESET`.
3. The board restarted into `STR8-N 2.0a25 B3 65C816`, kept `ABI 65C02 | 816E | 816N-VEC`, printed `EDU KIT       INCONCLUSIVE`, and returned to `B3>`. A separate `G F007` software reentry reproduced those lines. Read-only `T 3` returned to `B3>`.

No live flash dump or host readback was performed in this session, as requested. The installed sector identities rest on the on-board exact-image preflight and post-write verification messages; they do not have independent host byte-for-byte readback proof. The owner confirmed that board 2609 has no EDU attached; `INCONCLUSIVE` is the expected report for this setup. Owner-local logs, updater S19s, target BINs, and manifest are under `BUILD/board/a25-2609/`.
