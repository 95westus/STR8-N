# Alpha25 installation on W65C02SXB board 2512

Date: 2026-09-30. Board 2512 on COM4, FT245 serial `A10MQFLCA`. The user requested an alpha25 installation and made the board available at `B3>`.

The live E/F image was read twice independently, with both captures yielding SHA-256 `9717a340dbb3c9540db880fec8d9fd6717f64873aadbb0a899281c0e9f688df9`. E was all zero. F was byte-identical to the frozen alpha21 image (SHA-256 `3738eab501c50ef0470e9a81ef573b563da7dc656cc18a83573d16c9bd2e6e49`). The monitor reported `No config`. The alpha21 and alpha25 flash worker entry addresses matched, and the F-sector configuration/vector tail was byte-identical.

The [2512-specific builder](../tools/build_v2_a25_2512_guarded_ef.py) embedded the exact old and new E/F images in two RAM updaters. The [flash-model test](../tools/test_v2_a25_2512_guarded_ef.py) passed E/F ordering, exact preimage gates, cancellation/refusal, writes, verification, and preservation of B0/B2. It used the frozen alpha21 RAM worker and vector image for the pre-update stage. The [installer](../tools/install_v2_a25_2512_guarded_ef.py) checked FT245 serial identity, S19 integrity, the exact updater prompt, and on-board verification.

1. E update reported `B3:E exact; TYPE Y to repair>` before confirmation, then `B3:E VERIFIED; RESET`. A complete E readback was byte-exact for the alpha25 E image.
2. F update required that E readback, reported `B3:F exact; TYPE Y to update>` before confirmation, then `B3:F VERIFIED; RESET`. The board restarted into alpha25.
3. A complete independent E/F readback matched the alpha25 image, SHA-256 `8697674a8c2944e4a6d3a6dbe5cdf31d1ab11e037556278e716953f81c7b7d41`. E SHA-256 was `e7677fadba43c7f7e12e8dd41b235999f764d3553b8517e3dd27c9156d7d69ae`; F SHA-256 was `651fecf38f5ad1a82bd1254e64ea0361d92053ca0f6226a0d3daa2d7aa74b2a3`.
4. Software reentry with `G F007` printed `STR8-N 2.0a25 B3 65C02`, the unchanged ABI line, `EDU KIT       INCONCLUSIVE`, then `B3>`. Read-only `T 3` returned to `B3>`. The owner subsequently confirmed that board 2512 has no EDU attached; `INCONCLUSIVE` is the expected report for this setup.

Two receive-only physical RESET windows captured no bytes. The owner later shared two reset startup outputs showing the alpha25 banner, `EDU KIT       INCONCLUSIVE`, and `B3>`; those outputs were not captured by the host serial tool. The owner-local serial logs, exact readback BINs, updater S19s, and manifest are in `BUILD/board/a25-2512/`. No B0, B1, B2, RTC, or EDU storage write was requested by these installers.
